SIMPL_R / LIQUID · WELCOME

Sound is fluid.

I made LIQUID as a place to slow down and explore sound. It takes small pieces of a sample and lets you reshape them into something new. Start with a pad, move a knob, and see where it takes you.

  1. Enable audio. I’ve loaded junior_mesa.mp3 to get you started. Tap ENABLE AUDIO to connect the sound engine. Playback stays stopped.
  2. Play. Press PLAY for continuous sound, or hold the note pads to perform. Use LOAD AUDIO for your own WAV or MP3; a new file stays stopped until you play it.
  3. Shape it. Drag an encoder up or down. FLOW changes grain activity, MELT changes grain length, DRIFT adds motion, and RIPPLE changes articulation. Set pitch with PITCH; choose an effect with FX TYPE, then adjust SPACE and FX SYNC.
  4. Explore. Drag across the waveform to select a playback region. Try FREEZE to hold the grain position. Open FULL EDITOR for detailed audio, liquid and effect controls. Turn your phone sideways for pads on the right.
  5. Capture and save. Use LIVE CAPTURE to record an input, then stop to use it as a sample. RECORD OUTPUT records the processed sound; stop it, then export the WAV. The pad marked K / REC records a note performance; SEQ replays it.

Use headphones before enabling input monitoring. Audio stays in your browser. Reopen this guide anytime from MORE → QUICK GUIDE.
